FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Where can I order PaperTraq or check official marketplace availability?

Batch 01 reservations are currently coordinated directly through our official Instagram (@papertraq). Announcements regarding official marketplace storefronts (such as Tokopedia or Shopee) and companion app releases will be published first on @papertraq.

Does the person receiving my NFC tap need to install an app?

No. Modern iPhone and Android smartphones natively read the 13.56 MHz NFC tag and open your digital contact card or portfolio link directly, with an on-screen QR code as a universal backup.

What is the difference between PaperTraq Pro and Lite?

Both use the exact same 3.7" color e-paper display, NFC module, and Bluetooth tracker. Pro ($129) features a 42g 6061 CNC aluminum body, 30-day battery, buzzer, and includes the Smart Desk Dock; Lite ($79) uses a 19g matte polycarbonate shell with a 14-day battery and paracord lanyard.
